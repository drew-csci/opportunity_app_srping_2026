Frequently Asked Questions

What is Opportunity App?

Opportunity App connects high school and university students with meaningful volunteering, internship, and work opportunities. Organizations can post openings and students can discover, apply, and track their experience.

How do I create an account?

Click "Create account" in the navigation bar, fill out the registration form, and choose your role (student or organization). You'll be ready to go immediately.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

On the login page click "Forgot password?", enter your email, and follow the reset link sent to your inbox.

How long does it take to hear back after applying?

Response times vary by organization. You can send a reminder from your application detail page if you haven't heard back after a reasonable period.

How do I post an opportunity as an organization?

Log in with your organization account, go to your profile, and use the "Post Opportunity" button. Fill in the details and your listing will be live immediately.
